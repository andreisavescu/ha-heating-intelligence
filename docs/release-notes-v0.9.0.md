# Heating Intelligence v0.9.0 — Floor thermal performance and weather-normalised efficiency

## Highlights
- Downstairs/upstairs thermal comparisons with area-weighted temperature, observed cooling, heating response and demand hours.
- Visible room-area coverage so partial configuration cannot be mistaken for complete floor data.
- Weather-normalised daily gas comparison based on heating degree days, indoor–outdoor ΔT and solar radiation.
- Actual versus weather-model expected consumption, plus a normalised gas-use index referenced to median observed weather.

## Data requirements
- Room floor areas are optional and are entered using the existing **Set room floor areas** option.
- Weather model requires at least 21 completed days with gas consumption, outdoor temperature, ΔT and solar radiation.
- Until enough data is available, the card explicitly reports that the model is collecting data.

## Interpretation
These are comparative analytics, not a physical heat-loss coefficient or boiler combustion-efficiency measurement. Results are read-only and do not control heating equipment.
